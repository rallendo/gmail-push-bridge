## 2. Description of the Application

Gmail Push Bridge is an open-source macOS menu bar utility designed to establish a persistent, low-power IMAP IDLE connection to Google's mail servers, notifying local Apple Mail (`Mail.app`) when new mail arrives in your Gmail account.

The Application operates solely on your local device and does not provide hosted, managed, or remote email services.
