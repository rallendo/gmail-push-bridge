Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026 | Last Updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gmail Push Bridge ("we", "our", or "the Application"), developed by Rick Allendoerfer, accesses, handles, and protects your information when you use our open-source macOS utility.

1. 100% Local-First / Zero-Cloud Architecture

Gmail Push Bridge is designed strictly to operate locally on your Apple Mac:

  • Direct IMAP Connection: The Application connects directly from your Mac to Google's official IMAP mail servers (imap.gmail.com:993) via TLS. We do not operate any intermediate servers, cloud relays, web services, or databases.
  • Local Keychain Storage: OAuth tokens (access tokens, refresh tokens) and App Passwords are stored exclusively in your local macOS Keychain under service com.gmailpushbridge.app (kSecClassGenericPassword). They never leave your device.
  • Zero Tracking or Analytics: The Application does not contain any third-party tracking SDKs, analytics, crash-reporting telemetry, or advertising libraries.

2. Google User Data & OAuth Scopes

When you choose to authenticate using Sign in with Google (OAuth 2.0 with PKCE), the Application requests the following scopes:

  • https://mail.google.com/ (Restricted Scope: Full IMAP access) — Required by Google's IMAP server to perform SASL XOAUTH2 authentication and establish an IMAP IDLE connection to listen for real-time mailbox count notifications (EXISTS).
  • email — Used solely to retrieve your verified Gmail address to auto-populate the local configuration field.

Data Accessed vs. Data Read

The Application only reads the count of messages in your INBOX to detect when a new email arrives. The Application never reads, collects, inspects, stores, or transmits the body, subject line, sender, or attachments of your emails. When a new email arrives, Gmail Push Bridge signals your local Apple Mail client (Mail.app) via AppleScript to perform its native fetch directly from Google.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance Gmail Push Bridge's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Limited Use Requirements

In accordance with Google's Limited Use requirements:

  • No External Transfer: We do not transfer, transmit, or disclose Google user data to any external parties or servers.
  • No Advertising: User data is never used or transferred for serving advertisements, personalized marketing, or retargeting.
  • No Human Inspection: No human, including the developer, has access to or reads your Google user data.
  • No Model Training: Google user data is not used to train machine learning or artificial intelligence models.

4. Data Retention, Revocation, and Deletion

Because all data resides exclusively on your local device:

  • You can revoke the Application's access at any time through your Google Security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Clicking Sign Out (for OAuth 2.0, which also requests revocation at Google) or Remove App Password inside the Application immediately purges those credentials from your macOS Keychain.
  • Deleting the application removes all application binaries and configuration from your Mac.

5. Open Source Transparency

The complete source code of Gmail Push Bridge is public and open-source under the MIT license. You can inspect the network requests, Keychain handling, and AppleScript triggers at https://github.com/rallendo/gmail-push-bridge.
