2. Nature of the Application

Gmail Push Bridge is an open-source macOS utility that runs locally on your computer. It initiates an IMAP IDLE connection directly to Google servers and issues local AppleScript commands to macOS Apple Mail when new messages arrive. The Application does not provide cloud-hosted email services or remote intermediary servers.
